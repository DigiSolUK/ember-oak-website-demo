Solution blueprint

Make net client payments reconcile against gross Sage invoices.

The system records the job and gross invoice, detects the bank payment, calculates the withheld commission, then prepares a controlled Sage adjustment so the invoice is not left half-paid.

View Proposal

Recommended Workflow

1

Job completed

Engineer closes job with certificate, labour, materials, and client reference.

2

Gross invoice raised

Sage holds the full invoice value, preserving revenue reporting.

3

Net payment detected

Bank receipt is matched to invoice by reference, value, client, and expected commission rule.

4

Commission posted

The deducted commission is posted using the accountant-approved method.

5

Invoice settled

Cash plus commission adjustment clears the open invoice and keeps the audit trail intact.

What This Fixes

  • Invoices no longer remain incorrectly outstanding because the client has deducted commission.
  • Commission becomes visible as a cost or adjustment instead of being hidden in reconciliation notes.
  • Exceptions are escalated when the deduction does not match the agreed rate.
  • Bookkeeping becomes repeatable instead of manual month-end clean-up.
