If your electrical system is roughly 10 years old, it may require some improvements. Likewise, if your electrical system is over 30 years old, it will more than likely require electrical rewiring.

Failure to repair faulty wiring and electrics is extremely dangerous and can lead to fires and/or electric shock accidents.

If you are not sure whether your property is in need of a rewire, some of the signs to look out for include:

  • Burn marks on plug sockets
  • Fuses within the installation blowing regularly
  • Your fuse board having replaceable wire fuses.
  • Having to use extension and adaptors due to a lack of socket outlets
  • Lack of earth wires.

Based in London and Essex, our electrical engineers have years of experience in providing high quality rewiring in both commercial and domestic properties.

Whether you require a Full or Part Re-wire, our engineers will strive to complete the work as quickly and efficiently as possible, with minimum disruption. They will also be more than happy to answer any questions or queries you may have.

Full Rewire

A full rewire involves replacing all cables, accessories, light fittings and distribution equipment (Fuse Board) etc. Cables can be chased into the walls & completely hidden. Alternatively, cabling can be hidden in mini trunking or conduit, fixed onto the surface of the walls.

Part Rewire

A part rewire is simply the replacement of a section of an electrical installation, whether it‘s a fuse boxes, a light fitting, accessories or cables.
