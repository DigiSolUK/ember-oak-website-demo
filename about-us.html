Why Choose Us?

  • We Care! – We are a small local company with our reputation at stake. Meeting your expectations is our top priority. If you are not happy, we’re not happy.
  • We Deliver Quality! – We are confident in our work and offer a 2 year workmanship guarantee on all installation work we undertake, twice that of most competitors.
  • We Trade Responsibly! – We are fully insured with a £10 million public liability insurance policy, including professional indemnity. We are also vetted and registered with NICEIC, the UK’s most popular Electrical governing body.
  • We Supply Quality Products! –We research and handpick all our parts/products so that they will provide many years of reliable service. We do not specify products based on price alone, unlike most of our competitors who supply cheap, inferior parts. All parts/products supplied by us are tested and marked by either BSI (kitemark) or CE.
  • We’re Completely Independent! – We are not tied to any particular product, technology, or brand. We choose manufacturers based on their product rather than financial incentives they offer us.
  • We’re fully qualified! – Trained to the latest standards and amendments: BS7671.
  • We’re 100% Transparent! – All prices quoted are fixed and there are no hidden costs added at the end of a job.
