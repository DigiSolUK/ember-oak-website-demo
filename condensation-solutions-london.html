London Condensation Solutions: Dealing with Condensation

One of the first things that you need to know about condensation is that it’s most common from October until March. After this point, it begins to become less prevalent. Therefore, it’s important that you do something about it prior to “condensation season” starting. If you want to make sure that you are free of such problems, then you can use our expertise to end the problem once and for all.

Condensation Damp is the direct result of water vapor building up within the building. This can come from going for a long shower or bath, cooking and various other factors. What you will find is that the moisture begins to condense on cold surfaces, meaning that you can begin to notice problems such as:

  • Black spots and mold forming on the walls and ceiling.
  • A lack of quality air in the room, making it hard(er) to breathe.
  • Dampness in the room that can cause quality-of-life concerns.
  • Damaged walls that look awful and reduce the quality of aesthetic appeal in your home.
